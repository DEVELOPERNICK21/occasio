Thank you. Here is the information you asked for. The same details are in the Notes field of App Review Information.

1) Screen recording: attached. It was captured on a physical iPhone running the latest iOS, starting from launching the app, and shows: sign-up and sign-in (email and Sign in with Apple), creating a card and its web link, how a recipient opens it and the "Report this card" link, the subscription screen with each plan's title, length, price and the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links, restoring purchases, and Delete account and data.

2) Purpose and audience: Occasio helps people remember birthdays and anniversaries and send a personal greeting card (photo + message) as a web link. It is for anyone 13 or older who does not want to forget a date or send a generic text. A card takes about two minutes and the recipient needs no app or account.

3) Setup: No account is needed to create a card. On first launch the app asks for a date of birth once (13+); any date making you 13 or older works, for example 14/08/1990, and it is not saved. Then Create tab > who it is for > the moment > photo > message > Preview > Create the link. Sign-in is only needed for the Vault, History and subscriptions: Account tab > Continue with email, using the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields.

4) External services: Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging); RevenueCat with Apple In-App Purchase; Sign in with Apple; Google Sign-In; Resend (subscription confirmation emails); Vercel (card web pages and API). No AI services, ads or tracking.

5) Regions: the app works identically in every region. Only prices, which the App Store sets, vary. The app is in English.

6) Regulated industry: none. [CONFIRM: The app bundles five short sound effects and five animations; we hold the rights to use them.]

7) In-App Purchase: Occasio Pro auto-renewing subscription, Monthly $2.99 and Yearly $19.99 (unlimited cards, up to 5 saved people, auto-send); and one consumable, One Extra Wish, $0.99 (one more card in a month). The free plan includes 1 card per calendar month. To reach the purchase screens: create a second card in the same month, or Account tab > the subscription button, or the last step of the first-launch intro. Restore Purchases is on the Account tab.

User-generated content: cards are private one-to-one links, with no public feed, search, comments or messaging. Every card page has a Report this card link, and reported cards are reviewed and removed if they break our Terms. Account deletion: Account tab > Delete account and data.
