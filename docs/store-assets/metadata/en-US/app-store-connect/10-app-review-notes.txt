PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Occasio helps people remember birthdays and anniversaries and send a personal greeting card (photo + message) as a web link. It is for anyone 13+ who does not want to miss a date or send a generic text. A card takes about two minutes, and the recipient needs no app or account.

FIRST LAUNCH
1) A one-time age check (13+): enter any date of birth that makes you 13 or older, for example 14/08/1990. It is checked on our server and not saved. 2) A short intro (Skip is available). 3) The app opens on the Create tab.

MAIN FLOW (no account needed)
Create tab > who it is for > the moment > add a photo > write a message > Preview > "Create the link" > share. The link opens in any browser, for example https://occasio-greetings.vercel.app/c/demo-birthday-maya-1408 (a passcode-locked example uses code 1408).

SIGN-IN (only for Vault, History and subscriptions)
Account tab > Continue with email, using the demo account in the Sign-In Information fields. Sign in with Apple and Google are also offered.

PURCHASES
Occasio Pro, auto-renewing: Monthly $2.99, Yearly $19.99. One consumable: One Extra Wish $0.99 (one more card in a month). Free plan: 1 card per calendar month and 1 saved person. To see the paywall: create a second card in the same month, or tap the subscription button on the Account tab, or reach the last step of the intro. Restore Purchases is on the Account tab. Terms: https://occasio-greetings.vercel.app/terms  Privacy: https://occasio-greetings.vercel.app/privacy

USER CONTENT (1.2)
Cards are private one-to-one links. There is no public feed, search, comments or messaging; a recipient can only add a heart count. Every card page has a "Report this card" link (bottom right) that emails developernick1@gmail.com. Reported cards are reviewed and removed if they break the Terms, which prohibit abusive content.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Account tab > bottom > "Delete account and data" > Delete everything. This deletes the account, Vault, history, cards and photos at once. For Sign in with Apple accounts the app revokes the Apple token. The dialog explains billing and links to Manage subscription.

AUTO-SEND (Occasio Pro)
In the Vault a Pro user can arm auto-send for a saved birthday or anniversary. On the day the app sends a push asking them to review a prepared card; they have 2 hours to approve or cancel, then the share sheet opens so they send the link.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
Firebase (Google): Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging push. RevenueCat with Apple In-App Purchase: subscriptions. Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In. Resend: subscription confirmation emails. Vercel: hosts the card web pages and our API. No AI services, no ads, no tracking.

REGIONS
Identical features and content in every region. Only prices (set by the App Store) and the device language of system dialogs differ. The app is in English.

PERMISSIONS
Camera and Photos only when adding a photo. Notifications after sign-in, only to say a card is ready to review. No location, contacts, microphone or tracking.
