ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
Account tab > scroll to the bottom > "Delete account and data" > Delete everything. This immediately deletes the account, Vault, history, cards and photos. For Sign in with Apple accounts the app asks you to confirm with Apple once and revokes the Apple token. If you have a subscription, the dialog explains that billing continues until it is cancelled and offers a Manage subscription button.

AUTO-SEND (Occasio Pro)
In the Vault, a Pro user can arm "auto-send" for a saved person's birthday or anniversary. On that date the app sends a push notification asking the user to review a prepared card; they have a two-hour window to approve or cancel, then the system share sheet opens so they can send the link.
