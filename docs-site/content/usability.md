Run this **before Stitch**. Paper printouts, Figma wireframe mode, or the [Wireframes](/docs/wireframes) page on a phone are enough. Do **not** explain how the app works — watch where they get stuck.

## Recruit (3–5 people)

Aim for mix:

- 1–2 “forgetful adult child” (parents’ birthdays)
- 1–2 partner / peer gifting
- Optional: someone who recently received a digital card

India-first, smartphone users. Friends/family OK for this pass.

## Setup

- Show **mobile** wireframes only (Create → Share, Vault empty, Paywall, Recipient)
- Think-aloud; you take notes; no helping unless totally stuck (then note the hint)
- Capture: time-to-share, confusion points, whether they notice Vault nudge

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Create and share (Must story 1)

> “It’s your mom’s birthday tomorrow. Use this app to make her a digital wish and get a link you could send on WhatsApp.”

**Watch for:** template choice obvious? photo step clear? do they understand Preview vs Share? can they find Share/copy?

**Success:** reaches Share success without your help.

### Task 2 — Vault empty (Must story 2 entry)

> “You want the app to remember Mom for next year. What would you do?”

**Watch for:** do they find Vault? understand empty state CTA? expect auto-send immediately?

### Task 3 — Paywall (interrupt)

Show paywall after “Generate link” on free limit.

> “What is this asking you to do? What would you tap?”

**Watch for:** annual vs one-off clarity; trust / “will I be charged again?”

### Task 4 — Recipient

Show recipient card wireframe.

> “A friend sent you this link. What do you do next? Would you make your own?”

## After each session (5 min)

Ask:

1. What was this app for, in one sentence?
2. What was confusing?
3. Would you save a person so it sends next year? Why / why not?

## Decision rule

- **Proceed to Stitch** if ≥3/5 complete Task 1 unaided and can state the value roughly correctly
- **Revise wireframes** if Create→Share fails for majority, or Vault purpose is invisible
- Log findings under Changelog; update Wireframes before Phase 2.5
