## Personas

### 1. Ananya — forgetful but caring adult child (28)

- **Pain:** Misses or almost misses Mom’s birthday every year; feels guilty
- **JTBD:** When an important family date approaches, help me send something thoughtful automatically so I never scramble last-minute
- **Success:** Auto-send delivered; Mom opens the card; Ananya gets “Sent!” confirmation

### 2. Rohan — long-distance partner (24)

- **Pain:** Wants expressive digital moments; cash-light; opens card apps only when inspired
- **JTBD:** When I want to surprise my partner, help me make a shareable card in minutes without an account wall
- **Success:** Guest create → share link on WhatsApp in one sitting; later saves anniversary to Vault

### 3. Priya — recipient who becomes a sender (any age)

- **Pain:** Receives a beautiful card; wants to send one back / for her own people
- **JTBD:** When I open a shared Occasio link, help me make my own and save someone to my Vault
- **Success:** CTA → install/sign-in → first person saved (recipient→sender conversion)

## MoSCoW (MVP)

### Must

- Create & share a card (guest allowed)
- Add person to Vault + enable auto-send (auth + paid gate)
- Auto-send triggers, review window, deliver with WA→SMS→email fallback
- Subscribe to Personal/Family (store billing)
- Recipient opens shared card + path to save to own Vault / make own

### Should

- Creations history + reshare
- Unambiguous cancel / delete-person / delete-account
- Upcoming occasions list
- Soft-auth modal (not hard wall on create)

### Could

- One-off card unlock (₹49–99)
- Watermark upsell polish
- Notification permission education

### Won’t (v1)

- Video templates, group gifting, AI message assist, referrals, print/POD, contact scraping, dating features
