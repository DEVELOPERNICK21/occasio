Make every birthday, anniversary and thank-you feel like opening a gift.

Occasio turns a photo and a few words into an interactive card. Your person pops balloons, blows out the candles, unwraps a 3D gift and breaks the wax seal on a letter, then finds your photos and your reasons waiting inside. They open it from a simple link, so they don't need the app or an account.

WHY IT FEELS DIFFERENT
• A tiny adventure, not a static card: balloons, candles, a 3D gift and a letter that opens in 3D
• Five moments, each with its own look: birthday, anniversary, thank you, congratulations, just because
• Pip, a small mascot with a sense of humor (try tapping "No")
• Your photos, a jar of "reasons I love you" and a message in your own words
• Stars and collectible stickers turn opening a card into a little game
• A private link, with an optional 4-digit passcode

MADE IN A FEW TAPS
1. Pick the moment and who it's for
2. Add a photo and choose a frame
3. Write your words
4. Preview it, then share the link in any messaging app

NEVER MISS A DATE
Save the people who matter in your Vault with their birthdays and anniversaries. Occasio keeps track of the dates and can prepare the card for you on the day. You get a review window first, so you can approve, edit or cancel before anything is sent.

PRIVATE AND IN YOUR CONTROL
• Review before any auto-send, and cancel whenever you like
• Delete your account and your data from inside the app
• Your photos and words are shared only through the link you send
